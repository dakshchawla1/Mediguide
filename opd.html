Before You Visit
🆔
Carry Aadhaar Card
Mandatory for free OPD registration at all government hospitals. Carry original and a photocopy.
⏰
Arrive Early
Government OPDs give tokens from 7:30 AM. Arrive 30 minutes early for shorter wait time.
📄
Carry Previous Reports
Bring all previous prescriptions, blood reports, X-rays and scans for faster diagnosis.
🏥
OPD vs Emergency
OPD is for non-urgent conditions. For chest pain or breathlessness go directly to Emergency.
💰
Government = Free
Consultation and most tests are free at government hospitals. Jan Aushadhi sells cheap medicines.
💊
Bring Current Medicines
Carry all medicines you are currently taking so the doctor can check interactions.
