Before You Visit
Helpful Tips
🆔
Carry Aadhaar Card
Aadhaar is mandatory for free OPD registration at all government hospitals. Carry original and a photocopy.
⏰
Arrive Early
Government OPDs give tokens from 7:30 AM. Arrive 30 minutes before opening for a lower token number.
📄
Carry Previous Reports
Bring all previous prescriptions, blood test reports, X-rays and scan reports for faster diagnosis.
💊
Bring Current Medicines
Carry all medicines you are currently taking so the doctor can check for interactions and adjustments.
🏥
OPD vs Emergency
OPD is for non-urgent conditions. For chest pain, breathlessness or severe bleeding go directly to Emergency.
💰
Government = Free
OPD consultation and most tests are free at government hospitals. Jan Aushadhi counters sell cheap medicines.
