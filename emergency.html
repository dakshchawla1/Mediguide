🩹 First Aid Guide

What To Do Right Now

Tap any card to expand step-by-step first aid instructions.

01 💔
Heart Attack ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Call 108 immediately — do not delay even one minute
  • →Make the person sit or lie down in a comfortable position
  • →Loosen any tight clothing around the chest and neck
  • →Give one aspirin tablet to chew slowly if not allergic
  • →Stay with the person — do not leave them alone under any condition
02 🫁
Choking ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Ask if they can speak or cough — encourage forceful coughing
  • →Stand behind them and lean them slightly forward
  • →Give 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades
  • →Give 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich manoeuvre) if object remains
  • →Call 108 immediately if object is not dislodged
03 🩸
Severe Bleeding ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth or bandage
  • →Keep continuous pressure for at least 10 minutes without removing
  • →Do NOT remove cloth if soaked — add more cloth on top
  • →Elevate the injured area above the level of the heart if possible
  • →Call 108 immediately for deep wounds or arterial bleeding
04 ⚡
Electric Shock ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Do NOT touch the person directly with your bare hands
  • →Switch off the main power source at the fuse board immediately
  • →Use a dry wooden stick or rubber material to move them away
  • →Check breathing and pulse then call 108 immediately
  • →Start CPR if the person is unconscious and not breathing
05 🔥
Burns ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Cool the burn under cold running water for 10 to 20 minutes
  • →Do NOT use ice, butter, toothpaste, oil or any home remedies
  • →Carefully remove jewellery or clothing near the burn area
  • →Cover loosely with a clean non-fluffy sterile bandage or cloth
  • →Go to hospital immediately for large, deep or chemical burns
06 😵
Unconscious Person ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Call 108 immediately without any delay
  • →Check if the person is breathing normally — look, listen, feel
  • →Place them in recovery position — on their side with head tilted back
  • →Tilt head back gently and lift chin to open the airway
  • →Start CPR immediately if no breathing is detected
07 🐍
Snake Bite ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Keep the person calm and still — movement spreads venom faster
  • →Call 108 immediately and go to nearest government hospital
  • →Remove any rings, watches or tight clothing near the bite
  • →Do NOT cut the wound, suck the venom or apply tourniquet
  • →Note the colour and pattern of the snake if seen safely
08 🫀
CPR Guide ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Place heel of hand on centre of chest between nipples
  • →Push down hard and fast — at least 5 cm deep, 100 times per minute
  • →Allow chest to fully rise between compressions
  • →After 30 compressions give 2 rescue breaths if trained
  • →Continue until ambulance arrives or person regains consciousness
