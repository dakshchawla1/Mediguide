Immediate First Aid Protocols

Unresponsive / No Breathing

Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR)

  1. Place patient flat on their back on a hard surface. Check pulse.
  2. Position heel of hand in center of chest; interlock fingers.
  3. Push hard and fast: 100–120 compressions per minute at 2-inch depth.
  4. Do not interrupt compressions until paramedics take over.
Chest Pain / Left Arm

Heart Attack Protocol

  1. Sit the patient upright; loosen collar and tight clothing.
  2. Immediately dial 108 for ambulance dispatch.
  3. Chew 1 Aspirin 300mg tablet (if not allergic or bleeding).
  4. Never allow patient to walk, exert themselves, or drive.
Face, Arm, Speech, Time

Stroke FAST Emergency Test

  1. Face: Ask to smile. Does one side droop?
  2. Arms: Raise both arms. Does one drift downwards?
  3. Speech: Ask to repeat a simple sentence. Is speech slurred?
  4. Time: Dial 108 immediately. Every minute saves brain tissue.
Complete Airway Block

Choking First Aid

  1. Deliver 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades.
  2. Stand behind patient; form a fist right above the navel.
  3. Deliver 5 quick inward and upward abdominal thrusts (Heimlich).
  4. Alternate 5 blows and 5 thrusts until obstruction clears.
