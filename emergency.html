First Aid Guide

What To Do Right Now

Tap any card to expand step-by-step first aid instructions.

01💔
Heart Attack ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Call 108 immediately
  • →Make person sit or lie down comfortably
  • →Loosen tight clothing around chest and neck
  • →Give aspirin if available and not allergic
  • →Do not leave them alone — stay calm
02🫁
Choking ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Ask if they can speak or cough
  • →Encourage forceful coughing first
  • →Give 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades
  • →Give 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)
  • →Call 108 if object not dislodged
03🩸
Severe Bleeding ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Press firmly on wound with clean cloth
  • →Keep continuous pressure for 10+ minutes
  • →Do NOT remove cloth if soaked — add more on top
  • →Elevate injured area above heart level if possible
  • →Call 108 immediately for deep wounds
04⚡
Electric Shock ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Do NOT touch the person directly
  • →Switch off main power source first
  • →Use dry wood or rubber to move them away
  • →Check breathing and pulse — call 108
  • →Start CPR if person is unconscious
05🔥
Burns ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Cool burn under running water for 10 minutes
  • →Do NOT use ice, butter or toothpaste
  • →Remove jewellery near burn area gently
  • →Cover loosely with clean bandage
  • →Go to hospital for large or deep burns
06😵
Unconscious Person ▼

Tap to expand steps

  • →Call 108 immediately
  • →Check if they are breathing normally
  • →Place in recovery position (on their side)
  • →Tilt head back gently to open airway
  • →Start CPR if no breathing detected
